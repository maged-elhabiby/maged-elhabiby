## Research & robotics

My MSc in Electrical Engineering (Software Engineering specialization) focuses on **self-supervised multimodal learning for robotic perception and navigation**. I'm exploring predictive representations from mmWave radar, cameras, IMU, and GNSS, particularly for environments where GNSS is degraded or unavailable.

I hold a BSc in Software Engineering with a minor in Mechatronics from the University of Calgary.

## Selected work

**[VitalWaves](https://vitalwaves.ca/)** · Non-contact vital sign monitoring

Co-developed a mmWave radar system for estimating heart rate, breathing rate, and heart rate variability. My work focused on the Python signal processing pipeline, including phase extraction, adaptive filtering, and heartbeat detection. Our system achieved approximately **5 BPM mean absolute error** for heart-rate estimation.

**Research paper**

Co-author of *Improving mmWave Vital-Sign Monitoring with Segmented Phase-Difference Bandpass Demodulation*, accepted at **IEEE MECOM 2026**, based on our capstone research.
